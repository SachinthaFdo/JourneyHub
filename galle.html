04 — Southern Province

Galle

A fortified old town on the southern coast, its ramparts and cobbled lanes shaped by Portuguese, Dutch and British hands in turn. Walk the sea wall at sunset for the best view of the harbour.

  • 16th-century ramparts encircling the old town
  • Dutch colonial architecture and courtyards
  • A working lighthouse still in use
