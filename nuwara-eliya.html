02 — Central Highlands

Nuwara Eliya

Set high in the hill country, this town keeps a cooler climate and a distinctly colonial layout — tidy gardens, tea factories and hillsides striped with tea bushes stretching to the horizon.

  • Working tea estates open for tours
  • Cool evenings year-round
  • Gateway to Horton Plains National Park
