01 — Central Province

Sigiriya

A column of rock rising almost 200 metres out of flat jungle, topped by the ruins of a fifth-century palace. The climb passes mirror-polished walls, surviving frescoes and the carved paws of a long-vanished lion gate.

  • Ancient palace ruins at the summit
  • Ornamental water gardens at the base
  • Ideal visited early morning, before the heat
